A customer-support chatbot that routes requests into Bug Report, Platform Question, and Other Request categories.
Bug reports collect description, stepsToReproduce, and environment before creating a ticket.
The chatbot was evaluated across 10 test cases with an overall correctness score of 85%.
It uses FAQ-based responses for platform-related questions.
Unsupported requests are directed to the appropriate human support channel.
The workflow integrates with a bug-report tool to create and track reported issues.
